Reporting Specifications & Cloud Architecture
Attendance Percentage Formula
Attendance Percentage = (Number of Present Sessions / Total Applicable Sessions) × 100
  • Late Policy: Late arrivals are tracked distinctly and count as present toward total session attendance compliance.
  • Applicable Sessions: Cancelled sessions or sessions conducted prior to a student's enrollment are excluded from the denominator.
  • Division by Zero: When no sessions have taken place, the system handles the calculation safely and outputs 0.0%.
Amazon S3 & Scheduled Lambda Integration

Exports can be pushed to an encrypted Amazon S3 private bucket under the prefix: attendance-exports/YYYY/MM/.

  • Private & Secure: Reports are encrypted at rest with AES256 server-side encryption.
  • Presigned URLs: Downloads are authenticated using temporary presigned URLs expiring in 60 minutes.
  • Automated Lambda: A monthly AWS Lambda function triggered by Amazon EventBridge queries RDS and auto-archives reports to S3.
